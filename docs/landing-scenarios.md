# Landing-page proposal and illustration

The owner requested stronger racing copy, centered plan badge text, proposed Form Room daily targets of 1–3% and Inner Circle targets of 2–4%, and a 15-day chart.

The card ranges are visibly marked owner-proposed and unverified, with loss and no-guarantee wording. The compact card omits the allocation amount, while the linked trading page and purchase disclosures retain the separate membership/allocation terms and closed enrollment status. No financial terms snapshots, ledger logic, allocation gates or payout behavior are changed.

The hero chart is explicitly mathematical, not a historical record or a forecast. It plots simple cumulative percentage change over day 0–15 for assumed +2%, +4% and −2% daily outcomes: +30%, +60% and −30% respectively. It assumes no compounding and excludes fees and interruptions. There are no dates, simulated trades or invented Harry performance records. The member portal continues to use actual account records only.

Security copy describes implemented account access, manual deposit review and traceable records. It does not claim capital protection, immunity from war, stock-market movements or other disruptions.
